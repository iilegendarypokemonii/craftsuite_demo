Signal & Noise 2026 — findings
How social media talked about LLMs, Jan–Sep 2026 · Northbank Media Lab 
research desk
Illustrative report. All numbers come from a synthetic sample of 387 fictional posts. The report quotes no real people and 
describes no real product releases.
Summary
Agentic coding is now the main topic in the LLM conversation. Its share of the conversation grew 
from 9% in January to 20% in September. Reasoning & test-time compute fell from 18% to 6%. The 
focus moved from how models think to what models do.
• Agentic coding grew most: 9% of the conversation in January, 20% in September. It is the 
largest theme, with 15% of all posts.
• Reasoning & test-time compute fell: 18% to 6%. It went from the largest theme to one of 
the smallest.
• Benchmarks & evals also fell: 12% to 6%.
• Three themes grew slowly: Compute & energy and Multimodal & voice each went from 9% 
to 12%. On-device AI went from 6% to 8%.
• Prices & tokens is the most negative theme: 53% of its posts are negative. That is more 
than twice the 23% average.
• Regulation & policy peaked in July: 12% of the conversation.
Sample and method
The sample has 387 hand-coded posts from 6 platforms. The sample represents a much larger 
stream of posts. Each post has 1 of 10 themes and 1 sentiment label: positive, neutral or negative.
• Share of the conversation: the percentage of a month's posts that have a given theme.
• % negative: the percentage of a theme's posts that have a negative label.
• Tools: GridCraft calculated all percentages with COUNTIFS formulas. WordCraft typed and 
formatted this report.
